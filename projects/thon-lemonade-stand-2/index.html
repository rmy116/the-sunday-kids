Thon/Four Diamonds Lemonade Stand #2

The Sunday Kids ran a lemonade stand (this time in Newport Beach) to collect money for the Four Diamonds Fund associated with Penn State Dance Marathon. The Four Diamonds mission is to conquer childhood cancer by assisting children and their families through superior care, comprehensive support and innovative research.
