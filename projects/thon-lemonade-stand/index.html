Thon/Four Diamonds Lemonade Stand

The Sunday Kids ran a lemonade stand to collect money for the Four Diamonds Fund associated with Penn State Dance Marathon. The Four Diamonds mission is to conquer childhood cancer by assisting children and their families through superior care, comprehensive support and innovative research.
