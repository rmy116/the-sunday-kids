About Us

Who we are

Our purpose

We are a volunteer group aimed at getting young kids involved in their communities from a young age. There are no age requirements or hour requirements — our goal is simply to provide opportunities to allow kids to help in whatever way they can.

Our story

Turk and Nicole wanted to get their kids, Penelope, Kaia and Bodhi, more involved in their community. After searching, they found it extremely difficult to find any opportunities for their young kids, so they decided to start making them — and The Sunday Kids was born.
